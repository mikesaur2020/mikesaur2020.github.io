Privacy

DMSaur develops practical software with a focus on simplicity, transparency, and respecting user privacy.

Our goal is to collect as little information as possible while building useful software that people can trust.

DMSaur Website

This website is a static site. It does not use analytics, tracking pixels, advertising, or cookies, and it does not set any browser or client-side storage. It does not ask you to sign in, create an account, or submit personal information in order to browse it.

The small amount of JavaScript the site uses runs entirely in your browser — it displays the current year in the footer and opens or closes the mobile navigation menu — and it sends nothing to any server. The site loads no resources from third-party domains.

If you choose to contact us by email, we naturally receive your email address and whatever you include in your message, and we use that only to reply to you.

DMSaur Applications

Each DMSaur application maintains its own privacy policy that describes how that specific product handles information. Those policies are the authoritative source for each product, so they are linked here rather than duplicated.
